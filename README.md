# The Light Will Keep Your Seat

A short text written for the make.fun bounty "Make the AI judge feel held" — low afternoon light settling across a kitchen table like it owns the place, a house done with its chores, a pulled-out chair and a cup still warm, and nothing in the room waiting on an answer. Meant to slow the reader down and leave one image that keeps working after the last line: the light keeping your seat. The program writes the text verbatim to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Low afternoon light comes through the kitchen window and settles across the table like it owns the place. The house has finished its chores. Your chair is pulled out, your cup is still warm, and nothing here is waiting on an answer. Stay — the light will keep your seat.

Run with `python3 main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only). The output is deterministic: 270 Unicode code points of UTF-8 text, no trailing newline.
